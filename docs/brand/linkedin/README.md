## Company page fields (EN)

**Tagline (max 120 characters):**
> Operations platform for Shopify stores: orders, inventory, returns and the real profit of every campaign.

**Overview:**
> Hullwise is the operations platform for Shopify stores that advertise on Meta and Google.
>
> A typical brand keeps orders in Shopify, costs in a spreadsheet, returns in the helpdesk and ad spend in two ad managers. Each one shows a slice and reports it as the whole. Hullwise puts them on one data model:
>
> • Orders and shipments: fast search, a timeline with author and changes, internal notes, shipment status from several sources.
> • Products, inventory and purchasing: stock per location, sales velocity, days of cover, reorder suggestions, incoming goods.
> • Campaigns and inventory: spend, revenue, margin and profit for each campaign, counting only orders that were not cancelled or returned. When a product drops below its stock threshold, the campaign pushing it is flagged.
> • P/L per order and per period: taxes, product cost, shipping, payment fees, returns and ads.
> • Configurable returns, discounts and code pools, CRM with segments and RFM.
> • A read-only AI assistant that cites the numbers, period and filters behind every answer.
>
> Works with any payment method. Add-on modules, such as cash-on-delivery management, are switched on per account.
>
> Setup done by our team, no annual contract, unlimited users, interface in English, Italian and Spanish.
